The roads and buildings are coordinate-based vectors. Labels are rendered separately at screen resolution. The tree art deliberately keeps its pixel edges. This file works offline after download.

This is a review map, not a complete or surveyed campus plan. Every road geometry in the downloaded OpenStreetMap extract intersecting the buffer is retained, including unnamed roads. That does not prove every real road or Google Maps label is present.

  • OpenStreetMap university outline and road data, downloaded 17 October 2026. ODbL.
  • University Green and Carbon Audit (2020), printed page 30: approximate western outline and historical vegetation, fields and water. The combined review outline is used for the metric 500 m buffer; it is not a legal boundary.
  • Overture Maps release 2026-09-23.1: building footprints. Building sources include OpenStreetMap, Google Open Buildings and Microsoft. Some footprints are machine detected.
  • 91 named destinations checked against 99 visible Google Maps references on 18 October 2026; canonical academic names follow the university directory. Place details link to the individual reference. Alternate conflicting pins have a confirmation note. This is not an exhaustive Google Maps export or a field survey.
  • Tree symbols are decorative, not surveyed individual trees. No new streets or building locations are invented.
  • Small roads are visible at every zoom; zoom in for buildings and labels. Every retained name is in the searchable index. Count badges retain all grouped places; tap to list them or zoom in to separate their pins. Full names and aliases are searchable.
