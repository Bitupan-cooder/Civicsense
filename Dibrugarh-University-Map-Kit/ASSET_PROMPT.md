# Pixel tree artwork

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`. Transparent background requested.
Original: `assets/tree-canopy.png`. A 128 px nearest-neighbour packaging resize
is stored separately; the original is preserved. Roads, buildings, boundaries
and labels are rendered from geographic data, not generated by the image model.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one decorative tree sprite for a
> geographic pixel-game map. Create ONE small lush Assamese broadleaf tree
> canopy, viewed directly from above in orthographic top-down plan view,
> centered isolated on a truly transparent background. Charming 16-bit
> handheld RPG pixel art. Crisp square pixels, hard stepped silhouette,
> clustered leaf shapes in jade and emerald greens with warm lime sunlight
> from upper left, dark teal shade lower right. Very limited 8-color palette.
> Flat overhead canopy, no visible trunk, no isometric viewpoint, no landscape,
> no other objects, no words, no labels, no grid, no shadow beyond a tiny close
> shadow. Occupy 80 percent of the square canvas, transparent margin all
> around. This is a styling symbol only, not factual geographic content.
