Current state

Every screen in the PRD exists and is connected to the backend. Data loads, buttons work, and loading, error and empty states are handled.
The new screens are unstyled on purpose (plain HTML, no classes). Styling them is your part.
The database migration has run, and the whole flow was tested end to end on the live database. Complaints get categorised, routed to the right union post, tracked, replied to, confirmed or reopened, and merged as duplicates. Notifications arrive live.

Don't rename the IDs and classes listed in the guide.
Keep form controls controlled, with their value and onChange unchanged.
Keep the data-* and aria-* attributes; the tests rely on them.
Inside one app, links stay <Link>/<NavLink>. Between the student site and the console, use a plain <a>.
The campus map canvas must stay in the page's normal flow; the map data only loads when the canvas comes into view.
